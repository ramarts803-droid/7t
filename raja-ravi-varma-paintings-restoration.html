Raja Ravi Varma Paintings Restoration

Restoration of the paintings of Raja Ravi Varma, one of India’s most celebrated painters. Our director, Dr Madhan S, is the official restorer of Raja Ravi Varma Palace. Each work below shows the painting before and after conservation, and the restoration in progress.

Enquire about restoration Back to projects
Restoration Work 01Raja Ravi Varma

Going Out

Aloo Kharegat, a young woman from the Parsi community, is the subject of this work by Raja Ravi Varma, an artist who counted many Parsi families among his patrons. He shows her just as she is about to leave the house: wearing a red sari, she pushes a door open with one hand and holds an umbrella in the other.

Restoration Work 02Oil on canvas · 93.5 × 69 cm

Mother Preparing Vegetables

This oil painting from 1900 is the work of C. Raja Raja Varma (1860–1905), the younger brother of Raja Ravi Varma, who worked alongside him as a companion and collaborator during their years as artists.

Restoration Work 03Oil on canvas · 113 × 85 cm

Sakuntala

Painted in 1901 by Raja Ravi Varma (1848–1906), this oil on canvas draws on a scene from Kalidasa’s Sanskrit drama. Sakuntala sits beneath a palmyra palm, lost in thought, while in the distance the sage Durvasa approaches the hermitage.

Restoration Work 04Oil on canvas · 122 × 94 cm

The Lady with a Mirror

Raja Ravi Varma completed this oil on canvas in 1894. A woman stands before a mirror, arranging her long hair, and the artist’s rendering of the hairstyle is especially admired. The work was awarded a Governor’s gold medal.

Restoration Work 05Oil on canvas · 94 × 90 cm

Yasodha and Krishna

Often regarded as a pioneer of modern art in India, Raja Ravi Varma completed this work in 1901. He set the figures against a dark background so that their pale yellow tones stand out.
